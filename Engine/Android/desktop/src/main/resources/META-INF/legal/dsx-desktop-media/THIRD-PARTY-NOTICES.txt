DSX DESKTOP MEDIA — THIRD-PARTY NOTICES
=======================================

This directory is packaged inside the DSX desktop runtime JAR. It records the
additional libraries introduced by the first-party desktop audio/video/Lottie
renderers and carries their release-matched license and required notice text.

------------------------------------------------
Modules: javafx-base, javafx-graphics, javafx-media, javafx-swing
License: GNU General Public License version 2 with the Classpath Exception.
Source: https://github.com/openjdk/jfx21u/tree/21.0.8%2B2
Binary origin: Maven Central org.openjfx, pinned by SHA-256 in
OpenSource/Engine/Android/gradle/verification-metadata.xml.

The target-classified javafx-media binaries contain target-native media
components. Their exact upstream notices and license texts are included here:

- OpenJFX-LICENSE.txt
- OpenJFX-ADDITIONAL_LICENSE_INFO.txt
- OpenJFX-ASSEMBLY_EXCEPTION.txt
- OpenJFX-javafx.media-directshow.md
- OpenJFX-javafx.media-glib.md
- OpenJFX-javafx.media-gstreamer.md
- OpenJFX-javafx.media-libffi.md

DSX redistributes the Maven artifacts unmodified. The GStreamer notice names
the corresponding modified-library source location and removal instructions.
